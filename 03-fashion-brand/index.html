La Maison

Made by forty hands,
signed by one.

Founded in a third-floor workroom in the Marais in 1987, the Maison still cuts every pattern on the same oak table. Forty artisans work across two ateliers; each garment passes through every pair of hands before it is signed inside the collar.

We release two collections a year and never mark them down. What does not sell is unpicked and re-cut. Repairs are free for the life of the piece.
